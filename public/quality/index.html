Hard acceptance gates

The sequence assessor requires foot-anchor jitter ≤ 0.25 px, velocity error 0, speed delta ≤ 0.01, at most one animation-frame advance per capture, no one-shot backward frame jump, stable actor bounds, and no viewport clipping.

The reports are evidence, not a substitute for judgment. An agent or human still reviews every changed strip for natural weight, readable silhouettes, coherent camera movement, and accidental pose pops.
